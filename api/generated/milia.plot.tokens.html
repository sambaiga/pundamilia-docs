milia.plot.tokens#

Design tokens: the palette and the semantic colour roles every figure uses.

Figures refer to roles (PRIMARY, DANGER), never to palette positions, so re-binding a role here changes every figure at once. The first three palette entries carry meaning:

  • PALETTE[0] teal: the subject, the correct or reference case.

  • PALETTE[1] cyan: a second series, or a supporting one.

  • PALETTE[2] orange: the thing the reader should notice.

Grey (TEXT_MUTED) is reserved for reference lines and background points.

The WCAG contrast ratio of a colour against a background (1 to 21).

milia.plot.tokens.assign(names, centres, palette, highlight=None, accent=None)#

Colours for groups of an embedding, so that groups that lie next to one another differ most.

The groups take the first colours of the palette (without the accent colour, which is kept for the highlighted group). With eight groups or fewer, the assignment is the permutation of those colours that maximises the colour separation of every pair of groups weighted by the inverse distance of their centres; with more groups the palette is cycled.

Parameters:

  • names (Sequence[str]) – Group names.

  • centres (Sequence[Sequence[float]]) – Centre of each group, shape (groups, 2), in the order of names.

  • palette (Sequence[str]) – Categorical colours, in order.

  • highlight (str | None) – Name of the group drawn in the accent colour, such as a minority that stands apart.

  • accent (str | None) – Colour of the highlighted group, and the colour left out of the palette; the second palette colour when omitted.

Return type:

dict[str, str]

Returns:

Group name to colour.

Examples

>>> colours = assign(["1", "2", "3"], [[0, 0], [1, 0], [5, 5]], PALETTE, highlight="3")
>>> sorted(colours), colours["3"] == PALETTE[1]
(['1', '2', '3'], True)
