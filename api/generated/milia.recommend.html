milia.recommend#

Retrieval, baselines, case-based action recommendation and ranking.

Modules

action

Case-based recommendation: suggest the action that fixed the most similar past cases.

baselines

Baselines a retrieval method has to beat.

diagnostics

Diagnostics for whether entity-level retrieval makes sense.

equity

Equity of a selection or a burden: how evenly it falls on people or places, and selection under a bound.

ranker

Learning to rank: order items within each list by a score learned from their features.

ranking

Ranking cases, groups and models, with uncertainty on the ranks.

retrieval

Content-based retrieval: predict an entity's outcome from its nearest labelled neighbours.

uplift

Uplift: the change in an outcome that an action causes, estimated for each case from its features.
