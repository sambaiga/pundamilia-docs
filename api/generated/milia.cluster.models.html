milia.cluster.models#

Registered clusterers, one module per model (see milia.core.registry).

Modules

agglomerative

Agglomerative (hierarchical) clustering.

birch

BIRCH: clustering through a clustering-feature tree.

copkmeans

k-means with must-link and cannot-link constraints (Wagstaff et al., 2001).

dbscan

DBSCAN, with eps chosen from the k-distance elbow.

gmm

Gaussian mixture model.

hdbscan

HDBSCAN: hierarchical density-based clustering.

kmeans

k-means.

kshape

k-Shape: shape-based clustering with cross-correlation distance (tslearn).

minibatch

Mini-batch k-means, for large populations.

spectral

Spectral clustering on a k-nearest-neighbour graph, with an eigengap estimate of k.

tskmeans

Time-series k-means with Euclidean, DTW or soft-DTW distance (tslearn).
